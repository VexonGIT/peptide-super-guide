# Biohacker partner marketing guidance

Updated: 2026-10-28. Internal editorial guidance; not a published advertisement.

## Partners
- Soma Chems: retired. Do not include in new content or future campaign plans.
- Amino Tech: primary current partner. Referral URL: https://aminotech.shop/ref/485/
- Orbitrex: remains an active partner. Verify its approved destination before linking.

## Editorial approach
- Publish educational, evidence-based material about sleep, exercise recovery, metabolism, nutrition, longevity research, and performance science.
- Avoid directly featuring specific vendor products in general educational posts.
- Distinguish established human evidence, preliminary findings, and speculative mechanisms. Do not promise health outcomes, imply FDA approval, or recommend unapproved research chemicals for human use.
- No hidden advertising: whenever affiliate links, partnerships, compensation, or endorsements are involved, disclose the material connection clearly and conspicuously near the promotion, consistent with FTC guidance and platform policies.
- Do not use generic 'optimizer' or 'benefits' language to conceal a sales message or imply unsupported medical benefits.
- Review all content for accuracy, applicable advertising requirements, and partner terms before publication.

## Deployment
This document updates guidance only. It does not publish posts, change running services, or establish that any marketing automation has ingested this policy.
